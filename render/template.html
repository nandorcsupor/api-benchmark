    So, hype or worth it?

    Neither. It's a payback calculation.

    Worth it when
    • App servers are a top cloud cost
    • Hot paths burn CPU: JSON, parsing, pricing
    • High, steady traffic
    • p99 latency is a product feature
    Hype when
    • No product-market fit yet
    • The database is most of the bill
    • A cheaper fix exists: a cache, an index, a slow query
    • Your team can't maintain Rust
    Real world
    Grab, 2025. Go → Rust at the same traffic: 20 cores down to 4.5.
    Microsoft, 2025. Ported the TypeScript compiler to Go, not Rust: a port kept the existing structure, a rewrite would not.
